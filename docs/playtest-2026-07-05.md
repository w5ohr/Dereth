## OPEN — still needs work

### 🟠 #3 — Town walk-in portals sit on the footpath (UX / world layout)
Holtburg's two walk-in portals stand ~13u from the lifestone, directly on the natural walking line
to the tavern/shops — easy to blunder into (each = a surprise teleport to Nanto; at level 1 that's
lethal). Town compression parks them in the plaza; retail towns were full-size so walk-in portals
weren't on footpaths. **Fix direction:** nudge town portals to the plaza edge (same
minimal-displacement spirit as the building de-interpenetration solve), or add a step-back-confirm
ring. Deferred — needs a placement pass, a larger change than the other findings.

### 🟠 #6 — Endgame boss quests are offered at level 1
"Slay Bael'Zharon the Hopeslayer" and "Slay Martine the Fallen" appear **unlocked with `questLvl 1`**
for a fresh character — a new player's quest list opens with endgame bosses. Either `questLvl`
defaults to 1 when it can't be derived, or the gating chain for these boss quests is missing.
**Fix direction:** derive `questLvl` from the target creature's bestiary level, or gate the boss
quests behind their story arcs. (From the long-play pass, burst 4.)

**Root cause confirmed (8h regression pass):** `questLvl(id)` is `QUEST_LVL[id]||1`, and `QUEST_LVL`
is effectively unpopulated — so **every** quest reports recommended level 1 (verified: `bael_zharon`,
`gaerlan`, `kresovus`, `martine` and the *starter* quest `alfrin` all return `questLvl 1`, none carry
a `req`). The boss quests showing at level 1 is the visible symptom of quests having no per-quest
recommended level at all. Fix should populate `QUEST_LVL` (or derive it) for every quest, and add
`req`/arc gating to the endgame boss quests.

### 🟠 #7 — Trial Ground champion is a drudge ~50% of the time at level ≥72
`kcArenaStart()` picks the champion with `KINDS[Math.min(KINDS.length-1, floor(level/8)) + (random<0.5?0:1)]`.
`KINDS` has 10 entries (indices 0–9); from level 72 the base index is already 9, so the +1 branch reads
`KINDS[10]` → `undefined` → the `||"drudge"` fallback. A max-level lord's "champion foe scaled to your
might" is a 35 hp Drudge Slinker about half the time (verified live at simulated level 80: 7/20 picks
were drudge). **Fix direction:** clamp the final index (`Math.min(KINDS.length-1, base + roll)`), or
append stronger kinds so the roll can't overflow. (Castle Grand Expansion pass, `kcArenaStart`.)

### 🟠 #8 — Royal Menagerie pens are permanently empty (beasts never placed)
All six warded pens render base + posts + rings + name label but **no creature** — verified live after
5+ minutes with `gltfMonsters` fully loaded and `THREE.SkeletonUtils` present. Cause: `kcAddBeast`'s
deferred `place()` retry chain has two kill switches that both fire for any player who doesn't start
next to the castle: (a) `if(!pen.parent) return` — meant for "castle rebuilt", but `cullWorld()`
detaches pens beyond their 620u cull radius within ~0.25s, and the player spawns in Holtburg ~1.6 km
away, so the first retry finds `pen.parent === null` and the chain dies silently with no fallback;
(b) even without that, the retry window is only 10 × 700 ms ≈ 7 s, shorter than a typical model-pack
load. **Fix direction:** distinguish "culled" from "rebuilt" (e.g. keep retrying while the pen is
still registered in `kcMenagerie`), or place the beast lazily on first re-attach / first approach.
(Castle Grand Expansion pass, `kcAddBeast`.)

### 🟡 #9 — Castle floor hooks accept quest items
`kcUseHook`'s eligibility filter (`hsHookAccepts("floor",it) && it.stat!=="writ" && !it.scroll`) does
not exclude `stat==="quest"` — hanging your **Calling Stone** on a hook is accepted (verified live;
it left the inventory and became hook decor). Recoverable (take it back off), but a quest item parked
on one of 160 hooks in a private castle is easy to lose track of, and any quest logic that checks the
inventory won't see it. Same bug class as the quest-items-sellable fix shipped earlier today — the
vendor sell list now skips `stat==="quest"`; the hook filter should too. Worth auditing the housing
hook path (`hsHookAccepts` callers) for the same gap. (Castle Grand Expansion pass, `kcUseHook`.)

### 🟡 #12 — Castle guard kills are fully credited to the player (AFK farming)
*(Numbering note: #11 is the projectile-tunneling finding logged by the parallel session on its
branch — the numbers here start at #12 to avoid a collision when the branches merge.)*
Guard crossbow fire routes through the **player's** combat pipeline: `updateKcGuards` →
`applyHit` → `damageMonster` → `killMonster`, which unconditionally runs `player.kills++`, kill
streaks, XP (`gainXP`), bestiary unlocks and `questEvent` kill credit. Verified live: a guard
one-shot a drudge while the player stood idle → player gained the kill and 113 XP, and the combat
log printed "**You** hit the Drudge…". With 20 guards at ~140 dmg/s each, idling inside the walls
passively farms XP, kill-streaks and **slay-quest progress** from anything the mob streamer wanders
within 52u of a guard. Side effect of the shared pipeline: guard damage inherits the player's
modifiers — Blood Drinker/might buffs strengthen the guards, the player's crit chance
(Coordination) rolls for them, and **the player's Vitae weakens all 20 guards** by up to 40%.
**Fix direction:** give guard shots a neutral damage path (skip player mods), and pass an
"attacker" through to `killMonster` so NPC kills award no player XP/kills/quest credit (or a
reduced share by design — but quest credit almost certainly shouldn't tick). (`updateKcGuards`,
`applyHit`, `killMonster`.)

### 🟡 #13 — A Valstead deed-holder who loses guest status is locked out of their own home
Castle access (`castleAccess`: lord + `/grant` guests) and housing access (`hsAccessible`: deed) are
independent. A guest can buy a Valstead cottage (300,000 py + Writ, level 20), then lose their grant
(`/grant` revocation) — verified live: `/house recall` drops them at their own door, and
`enforceCastleAccess` immediately shoves them ~113u away, outside the moat. They keep paying-grade
property (deed, storage, hook items) they can never re-enter; the only exit is abandoning the deed.
**Fix direction:** let `castleAccess()` also admit holders of a deed whose house has the
`castle:true` flag, or refuse/warn on `/grant` revocation while the guest holds a Valstead deed.
(`castleAccess`, `hsAddCastleDwellings`, `enforceCastleAccess`.)

### 🔵 #10 — three.js console warn flood: `toNonIndexed(): BufferGeometry is already non-indexed`
384+ identical warnings piled up during the castle visit — something in the model-building path
(likely `buildItemModel` for the 160 hook display pieces) calls `toNonIndexed()` on geometry that is
already non-indexed. Harmless but noisy (drowns real warnings) and does redundant work per model.
**Fix direction:** guard with `if(geom.index) geom = geom.toNonIndexed()`. (Castle Grand Expansion pass.)

### 🔵 #11 — Projectile collision is a per-frame point test → fast shots tunnel at low framerates
`updateProjectiles` (index.html ~15211) advances a projectile by `p.vx*dt` then tests the **new**
position against each monster: `Math.hypot(p.x-m.x, p.z-m.z) < m.r+p.r && Math.abs(p.y-1)<1.8`.
There is **no swept/segment check** between the previous and current position, so when a projectile's
per-frame step exceeds the target's collision radius (~0.9u) it passes straight through without
registering a hit. At 60fps an arrow (`pspd` 75) moves ~1.2u/frame and lands inside the radius → hits
fine; but on a framerate dip (dt clamped to 0.05 → ~3.75u/frame) or with a faster projectile, the
discrete positions straddle the target and the shot is lost. Reproduced in the harness: arrows spawn
and consume ammo correctly, but under sparse (~software-WebGL) frames a dummy 6u ahead took **zero**
damage across many shots because every arrow tunneled past it. **Not visible at normal framerates**
(melee and 60fps ranged both connect) — low severity, but a real robustness gap. **Fix direction:**
sweep the projectile's travel segment against monster circles each frame (closest-point-on-segment ≤
`m.r+p.r`), or sub-step long moves. Same code path covers arrows *and* spell bolts.

### 🔵 #16 — `damageMonster` doesn't guard NaN / negative damage (→ un-killable or self-healing mobs)
`damageMonster(m,dmg,crit)` (index.html ~9455) applies `m.hp-=dmg` with no validation, then
`if(m.hp<=0) killMonster(m)`. Two bad-input cases (verified in-harness):
- **NaN** — `damageMonster(m, NaN)` sets `m.hp = NaN`; `NaN<=0` is false, so the mob never dies and,
  since `NaN - x = NaN`, **no further damage can ever kill it** — a permanently un-killable monster
  (also breaks any HP-bar/`m.hp/m.mhp` math downstream).
- **Negative** — `damageMonster(m, -50)` runs `m.hp -= (-50)` → the mob is **healed** (100→150, past
  its max HP).

**Not reachable in normal play** — `applyHit` builds damage from positive terms × positive multipliers,
so it's always a finite ≥0 number; this is defense-in-depth, same class as #15 and the fixed P3-1. But
`damageMonster` is the central hot path for *all* damage (melee, arrows, spell bolts, DoTs, guards),
so a single upstream NaN/negative — a future item with a bad stat, an undefined skill value, a
divide-by-zero in a multiplier — would silently produce a stuck or self-healing monster that reads as
a mysterious "invincible enemy" bug. **Fix direction:** clamp at entry — `dmg = Math.max(0, +dmg||0)`
in `damageMonster` (and/or in `applyHit`) — cheap, and it immunises every damage source at once.
(Found via an edge-case/error-path pass; no new content had merged to test.)

### Graphics / world geometry
- **✅ G1 — Lifestone read as an oversized "insectoid" crystal.** The crystal geometry was fine
  (native 3.6m upright spire); the "wider than tall" reading was the group bbox contaminated by the
  flat clearing disc (r 4.6 → 9.2 diameter). Fix (`buildLifestone`): normalise the crystal to a

  2.15w × 2.6h × 2.02d, base on ground.
- **✅ G2 — Overworld monsters rendered buried ~90u underground** (19 of 32 far mobs). A mob's mesh-y
  was only set to `groundY` while simulated (`SIM_RADIUS`=95); un-simulated ones kept spawn-y=0.
  Masked at old ⅓ scale, exposed at 1:1. Fix: `spawnMonster` seats mobs at `groundY(x,z)`, plus a
  sim-skip safety net planting far idle mobs each tick. Verified 0 buried (was 19).
- **✅ G3 — PK altars buried ~28u underground.** `addPKAltar` used `y=0` instead of `groundY`. Fixed;
  both Holtburg altars now seat on the terrain.
- **✅ G4 — Untextured glaring-white building panel at Shoushi.** Some AC building surfaces carry a
  near-white "unset texture" placeholder that rendered as a blank white panel under PBR sun. Fix
  (`tbBuildMesh`): tone any untextured near-white surface (all channels > 190) to muted stone
  `0x9c9078`, roughness 0.94. Verified 0 glaring-white surfaces at Shoushi.

### Economy / gameplay
- **✅ Economy — infinite-money loop at trade-note / low-value vendors** (🔴 HIGH). Buy Trade Note
  (100) for 2p, sell back for 3p → +1p/cycle forever (the 2p buy-clamp floor undercut the generic
  sell value). Fix: trade notes sell at face−1, and every vendor-bought item is stamped with its buy
  price (`bp`) which hard-caps its resale at `bp−1` — closes the whole exploit class (also caught a
  Ginseng/comp variant). Verified live (net negative over cycles). Shipped on `fix-money-exploit`.
- **✅ Death — dead player could act during the 1.4s death fade.** `interact()` had no `player.alive`
  guard, so pressing E on the death spot instantly recovered your own corpse, erasing the corpse-run
  penalty. Fix: gate `interact()` on `player.alive`. Shipped on `fix-playtest-findings`.
- **✅ Magic — 881 vendor spell scrolls were unlearnable curios** (🔴 HIGH). Retail scroll items in
  ACE vendor stock fell through `acItemRow` to 📦 trophy curios — none scribeable, and since acStock
  *replaces* generic stock, ACE-stocked towns sold no learnable scrolls at all. Fix: (a) `acItemRow`
  maps scroll names to real scribeable scrolls when the spell exists in `SPELLBOOK`; (b) every ACE
  archmage/scrivener also carries the standard 30-scroll shelf. Verified end-to-end: buy → scribe →
  train school → cast.
- **✅ Inventory — quest items were sellable** (🟡). The sell list offered quest items (Calling
  Stone, Letter From Home) — selling one could strand its quest. Fix: skip `stat==="quest"` in the
  sell list (as scrolls already are). Shipped on `fix-playtest-findings`.

### Code robustness
- **✅ P3-1 — skill helpers crashed / polluted the save on an invalid skill key.** `skillState()`
  persisted a junk `{t:0,xp:0}` for any key, and `skillBase`/`skillValue`/`skillEff`/`trainSkill`/
  `specSkill` dereferenced `SKILL_BY_KEY[key]` (`.d`/`.tc`/`.sc`/`.base0`/`.fam`) unguarded — an
  unknown key (stale save, renamed skill, bad caller) threw `Cannot read properties of undefined`.
  Not reachable via the UI (keys always come from `SKILLS_DEF`), so defense-in-depth. Fix: guard all
  six helpers — unknown key now returns safe defaults (0 / 1× / false) with no throw and no save
  pollution; valid keys unaffected (verified regression-clean). Shipped in PR #76.

## Candidates investigated → NOT bugs (harness test-input artifacts)

From the systems pass — each traced in source and confirmed as a test-setup mistake, not a game bug:
- **Missile ammo "not consumed" / 0-count fires** — read the wrong ammo stack (crossbow quarrels vs
  bow arrows) and forced an impossible `count:0` stack; `fireArrow` does consume ammo and
  `takeFromInv` removes a stack at count 0.
- **Attr buff "no-op"** — the Creature school was never trained (out of *skill credits*, which come
  from leveling, not granted XP), so `canCast` correctly rejected the cast. Confirmed working when
  force-trained.
- **Tinker "no-op" on a bow** — `MAT_APPLY['Steel']` resolves to **armor** (armor loop wins), so a
  Steel bag on a weapon is a no-op by design; weapon tinks need a weapon-class material (Titanium).
- **"Stuck" cast** — headless frame sparsity + `dt` clamp (0.05/frame) means 2.5s wall ≪ 0.4s cast
  time in a heavy scene; the loop is alive, the cast just hadn't finished. Test effects via the
  instant `executeSpell` path.

## Test-harness notes (not game bugs)

- **`WrongDocumentError … pointer lock`** — thrown by `cEl.requestPointerLock()` in `startGame()`
  under headless (no user gesture). Harmless; runs inside a click handler in a real browser.
- **Boot is heavy** — `startGame()` → `buildWorld()` builds the ~4M-vertex terrain (GROUND_SEG 2000)
  synchronously (~30s under software WebGL). Real-GPU cost is negligible.
- **Sparse headless frames + `dt` clamp** — `loop()` clamps `dt=min(dt,0.05)`; under SwiftShader with
  a heavy scene, game-time advances far slower than wall-clock, skewing time-based tests (cast bars,
  projectile travel). Verify such effects via instant paths or give generous waits.
- **Adaptive graphics downgrade** — the ~0.5fps headless loop trips the "frame rate dipped below 30
  → step graphics down" system, so some earlier "over-bright/washed-out" observations were captured
  in a downgraded state, not true bugs. Re-verify any *lighting/texture-wash* finding at forced-High
  on real hardware. (Structural/geometry findings — buried-y, scale — are quality-independent.)
- **Out-of-loop `renderComposite()`** with `running=false` corrupts the post-processing render
  targets → black viewport. Harness artifact, recovered by reload; not a game bug.
