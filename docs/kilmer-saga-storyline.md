# The Kilmer Saga — "The Tenfold Crown"

A ten-year serialized storyline and monthly event schedule for Dereth, continuing Asheron's Call's
canon history with the emergence of **Lord Kilmer**, builder of **Castle Val Halla** and Monarch of the land.
Companion to `asherons-call-events-reference.md` (event framework) and `asherons-call-world-reference.md` (places).

---

## Part I — The story so far: Asheron's Call canon history

### The deep past (Empyrean era)
- **The Falatacot** — the eldest human-adjacent civilization; blood magic, leyline temples, dragon-worship.
  Their severed temple-sites still dot Dereth. *(This matters later.)*
- **The Dericost** — a winter kingdom whose nobility cheated death and became the undead; their vaults
  sleep under Stonehold and the ice-city of **Frore**.
- **The Yalain / Empyrean golden age** — the tall, long-lived Empyreans master portalspace. **Asheron Realaidain**
  is their greatest portal-mage. Their war with **Bael'Zharon**, a Hopeslayer elevated from their own ranks,
  ends with Bael'Zharon imprisoned inside crystal soul-shards.
- **The Olthoi cataclysm** — insectoid Olthoi pour through a portal experiment gone wrong and annihilate
  Empyrean civilization. Asheron's last desperate working shields the survivors but tears portalspace open —
  and begins pulling in humans from the world of **Ispar**.

### The Isparian age (game launch, 1999 → 2017)
1. **Arrival & the war for daylight** — Aluvians, Gharu'ndim, and Sho arrive through portal storms, escape
   Olthoi slave-pens, and found the towns of Osteth (Holtburg, Yaraq, Shoushi and their realms).
2. **Arc 1 — Bael'Zharon's return (2000–01)** — cultists erode the soul-shards; at the **Shard of the Herald**
   event players famously defended the last shard (one server, Thistledown, held). The Hopeslayer walks;
   Shadows invade; he is cast down but never destroyed.
3. **Arc 2 — The Virindi Schism** — the masked, hollow Virindi split into warring factions; their
   "Singularity" experiments on human minds.
4. **Arc 3 — Gaerlan (2003)** — an imprisoned Empyrean elementalist is accidentally freed; his sky-citadel
   falls to adventurers.
5. **Arc 4 — The Olthoi Queen's revenge** — the Ancient Queen strikes at Asheron directly; her brood war
   re-opens the hives.
6. **Arc 5 — Falatacot & the Burun (2004)** — amphibian Burun hordes serve the returned **Blood Matriarchs**;
   Falatacot leyline magic re-enters the world.
7. **Arc 6 — The Viamontian invasion / Throne of Destiny (2005)** — King **Varicci II** of Viamont lands armies
   on Dereth's new continents; his pet horror **Grael, the Arm of Destruction** is unleashed and barely re-bound.
8. **Arc 7 — Aerbax's plot (2007–10)** — the renegade Virindi **Aerbax** elevates "Prodigal" creatures and
   twists heroes' deeds toward its own apotheosis.
9. **Arc 8 — T'thuun (2014–16)** — the final year-long arc: the drowned god of the Slithis rises from the
   deep; Moarsmen and Slithis tide over the coasts before the Light drives it back — barely.
10. **The Great Fading (in-lore for Jan 31, 2017)** — portalspace convulses; the lights of Dereth go dark to
    the outside. The chronicles simply stop.

### The Interregnum — and the emergence of Kilmer
Generations pass inside the Fading. Asheron is gone — his castle courtyard tended only by Chafulumisa and
Fianhe. Towns endure behind their lifestones, but the Town Network gutters, criers fall silent, and the old
protections rot.

Out of that silence comes **Kilmer** — an Isparian descendant, an adventurer in the old mold. The chronicles
credit him with three deeds in the dark years:

1. **He reopened the roads.** Kilmer's coin and sword-hand restored the Town Network hub, re-lit the
   lifestones, and put criers back on every corner.
2. **He broke the Shadow remnant** that had squatted in the Aluvian heartland since the Fading, scattering
   the cult at the gates of Holtburg.
3. **He raised Castle Val Halla** — a colossal moated keep on a flattened plateau west of Holtburg, with the
   town of **Valstead** at its feet — and threw its gates open as a refuge.

For this the three old capitals — Holtburg, Yaraq, Shoushi — swore the **Concord of the Three Realms**, and
Kilmer was acclaimed **High Monarch of Dereth**, the first crown the land has ever accepted. New Viamont's
spire-towns signed on warily; the Tumerok and Lugian nations sent observers, not oaths.

What no chronicle records — *yet* — is **why the plateau under Castle Val Halla was already flat**, and why
crops, coin, and luck have run so unnaturally kind in Valstead ever since. That secret is the spine of the
ten-year saga.

## Part II — The saga in one page

**The Tenfold Crown** is ten year-long arcs. Each year has a villain, a build-up, a Harvestgain-season
climax, and consequences that persist. Two revelations restructure the saga:

- **Year 5 twist:** Castle Val Halla stands on a severed **Falatacot temple-crown** — a blood-leyline root.
  Kilmer's golden reign has been quietly *funded* by the oldest magic in the world, and the things that made
  that magic want it back.
- **Year 8 revelation:** Asheron did not abandon Dereth. He sealed himself into the edge of portalspace to
  hold the Veil shut through the Fading — and the seal is failing. What waits on the far side is **T'thuun**,
  who never stopped rising.

**The final event (Year 10, Month 120 — "The Last Light of Val Halla"):** the only power on Dereth old enough
to re-seal T'thuun is the Falatacot root under Kilmer's own castle. The Monarch must burn his source of
power — throne, fortune, castle-light and all — to save the land. He does. The plateau goes dark, T'thuun is
sealed beneath the Vesayen deeps, and a land that crowned Kilmer for his castle re-crowns him for the loss
of it. The saga ends where it began: a monarch, acclaimed — this time by deed alone.

### The Derethian year (month wheel used below)
Spring: **Morningthaw · Seedsow · Leafdawning · Thistledown**(festival) — Summer: **Verdantine · Solclaim** —
Autumn: **Leaffall · Harvestgain**(climax season) **· Leafcull** — Winter: **Coldeve · Frostfell**(festival) **· Snowreap**.
Recurring annual festivals (non-story months carry them): **Thistledown Festival**, **Harvestgain Fair**, **Frostfell Festival**.

## Part III — The monthly event schedule (120 months)

Event types follow the taxonomy in `asherons-call-events-reference.md` §3:
*beat* (story chapter) · *invasion* · *boss* (world-boss climax) · *seasonal* (world change) · *festival* ·
*item* (new gear line) · *explore* (new land/dungeon). Every month ships one event on the 5-beat skeleton:
**Crier rumor → clue trail → destination → climax → reward + world change.**

- Drives entirely off the `EVENT_CALENDAR` framework sketched in `asherons-call-events-reference.md` §5 —
  each row above is one calendar entry (`arc`, `chapter`, `kind`, `crierRumor`, `clue`, `reward`, `worldState`).

  town-under-siege state (Y4M39), rift/portal-storm spawner (Y8), coastal water-level state (Y9),
  arc-progress save + relic tracking for the Month-116 veterans' ceremony.
- **Cadence:** one calendar row per real month, or accelerate on the in-game season clock (events doc §5).
- The 5-beat skeleton (rumor → clue → destination → climax → reward + world change) applies inside every row.
